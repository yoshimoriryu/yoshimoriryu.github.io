Service Log privacy policy

Effective 25 October 2026

In short: Service Log does not collect any personal data. It has no account, no server, no analytics, no ads, no tracking and no third-party code. What you enter stays on your iPhone.

Who we are

Service Log: Car & Motorbike (“the app”) is made by Hamzah Chairil Salim, an individual developer based in Indonesia (“I”, “me”). Contact: hamzah.chairil98@gmail.com.

Data the app collects

None. The app does not send any information about you or your use of the app to me or to anyone else. It contains no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking software, and it works without an internet connection.

Data stored on your iPhone

  • Your vehicles (name, type, make, model, year, plate if you enter it, odometer), service records, costs, shops, notes, reminder rules and fuel fill-ups. They are kept in a database inside the app on your iPhone, with your settings in the app's own storage.

This data never leaves your iPhone through the app. I cannot see it. It is included in your own iPhone backups (iCloud Backup or a computer backup) if you have them turned on; those backups are handled by Apple under your Apple Account, not by me.

Device permissions

  • Notifications: service reminders are local notifications, scheduled on your iPhone a week before and on the day a service is due. No push server is used.

You can change these permissions at any time in the iPhone Settings app.

Purchases

The Pro unlock is a one-time in-app purchase processed by Apple. I do not receive your name, Apple Account or payment details. The app only asks Apple whether Pro has been bought on your Apple Account, to unlock it and to restore it. Apple's handling of purchases is described in the Apple Privacy Policy.

Ratings

The app may show Apple's standard rating prompt. Any rating or review you give is handled by Apple; the app does not see it.

Sharing and export

If you use a share or export feature, the app hands the item to the iOS share sheet and you choose where it goes. Nothing is shared without your action.

Feedback by email

“Send feedback” in Settings opens a draft in your own mail app, addressed to hamzah.chairil98@gmail.com, with the app name, app version and iOS version in the subject. Nothing is sent unless you send the email yourself. If you do, I receive your email address and what you wrote, and use them only to reply to you. Ask me to delete your emails at any time and I will.

Keeping and deleting your data

Delete a record or a vehicle in the app to remove it. Deleting the app removes everything it stored. Because I hold no data from the app, there is nothing for me to delete on a server.

Children

The app does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If the app ever starts collecting data, this policy and the App Store privacy label will be updated before that version is released.
