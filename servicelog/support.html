Service Log: Car & Motorbike support

Car and motorbike maintenance history, reminders and fuel log.

What the app does

Service Log keeps the maintenance history of your car or motorbike and tells you what is due next. Set reminders by distance or time (for example, oil every 2,500 km or 3 months, whichever comes first), log services with costs, and log fuel fill-ups to see consumption.

Free: One vehicle with unlimited service records, reminders and fuel logging.
Pro (one-time purchase, $2.99 in the US; local prices vary, no subscription): Unlimited vehicles, fuel statistics, a yearly cost report and PDF export of the service history.

Works offline. No account, no ads, no tracking, no data collected. iPhone, iOS 17 or later.

Restore your Pro purchase

On a new iPhone, or after deleting and reinstalling the app:

  1. Sign in to the App Store with the same Apple Account you used to buy Pro.
  2. Open Service Log, tap Settings, then Restore purchase in the Pro section. The paywall screen has the same button.

Pro is a non-consumable purchase, so restoring is free. If Family Sharing is on, family members can use it too. If Restore finds nothing, check that you are signed in with the right Apple Account and have an internet connection, then try again.

For refunds, Apple handles all payments: request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Questions

How does it know when a distance reminder is due?

It learns your average daily distance from the odometer readings you enter (services and fill-ups) and estimates the date. The more often you update the odometer, the better the estimate.

Are the default intervals right for my vehicle?

They are common rules of thumb, with shorter intervals for motorbikes. Check your owner's manual and change any interval in the vehicle's reminders.

It says my odometer value looks wrong.

Service Log warns when a reading is lower than an earlier one, which is usually a typo. Check the number; you can still save it if it is correct (for example after an odometer replacement).

How do I show the history to a buyer?

With Pro, export the vehicle's full service history as a PDF and share it from the share sheet.

Why can I only add one vehicle?

The free version has one vehicle with unlimited records. Pro, a one-time purchase, unlocks unlimited vehicles.

Where is my data stored? Can I move it to a new iPhone?

Only on your iPhone. It moves to a new iPhone when you restore from an iPhone backup (iCloud Backup or a computer backup) or use Quick Start. There is no account or sync service.
