Paint & Tile Calculator support

Paint, wallpaper and tiles for a room, with an optional LiDAR scan.

What the app does

Paint & Tile Calculator works out how much paint, wallpaper and tile a room needs. Enter the room size in metres or feet; doors and windows are subtracted, coats and primer are counted, and it picks the cheapest mix of cans. Pro adds wallpaper rolls (with pattern repeat), floor tiles with waste per layout, skirting boards, cost totals, unlimited rooms, and a LiDAR room scan on supported iPhones.

Free: One room with the full paint calculator and can picker.
Pro (one-time purchase, $3.99 in the US; local prices vary, no subscription): Unlimited rooms and projects, LiDAR room scan, wallpaper rolls, floor tiles and skirting, and cost totals.

Works offline. No account, no ads, no tracking, no data collected. iPhone, iOS 17 or later.

Restore your Pro purchase

On a new iPhone, or after deleting and reinstalling the app:

  1. Sign in to the App Store with the same Apple Account you used to buy Pro.
  2. Open Paint & Tile, tap Settings, then Restore purchase in the Pro section. The paywall screen has the same button.

Pro is a non-consumable purchase, so restoring is free. If Family Sharing is on, family members can use it too. If Restore finds nothing, check that you are signed in with the right Apple Account and have an internet connection, then try again.

For refunds, Apple handles all payments: request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Questions

Why don't I see the room scan?

The scan needs an iPhone with a LiDAR scanner (Pro models from iPhone 12 Pro on) and the Pro unlock. On other iPhones, enter the measurements by hand; every calculation still works.

Where do I find the coverage figure?

On the paint label, usually as square metres per litre or square feet per gallon. Enter it as printed; the app converts units for you.

How accurate are the results?

They are estimates based on your measurements and the label's coverage. Walls that are rough or porous take more paint. Buy a little extra for touch-ups.

Why can I only add one room?

The free version has one room with the full paint calculator. Pro, a one-time purchase, unlocks unlimited rooms and the other calculators.

Where is my data stored? Can I move it to a new iPhone?

Only on your iPhone. It moves to a new iPhone when you restore from an iPhone backup (iCloud Backup or a computer backup) or use Quick Start. There is no account or sync service.
