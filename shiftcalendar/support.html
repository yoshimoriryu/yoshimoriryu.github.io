Shift Calendar: Rota Planner support

Rotating shift patterns at a glance, with widgets and pay totals.

What the app does

Shift Calendar shows a rotating rota at a glance. Pick a preset (4 on 4 off, Panama 2-2-3, DuPont, Continental 2-2-2, 24/48 and more) or build your own cycle, set the start date, and it repeats for years. Night shifts that end the next morning are handled correctly. Swap a shift, mark a day off or add leave without changing the pattern.

Free: One schedule with presets, your own shift types and day changes.
Pro (one-time purchase, $3.99 in the US; local prices vary, no subscription): Home Screen widgets, reminders before shifts, paid hours and a pay estimate, and multiple schedules.

Works offline. No account, no ads, no tracking, no data collected. iPhone, iOS 17 or later.

Restore your Pro purchase

On a new iPhone, or after deleting and reinstalling the app:

  1. Sign in to the App Store with the same Apple Account you used to buy Pro.
  2. Open Shift Calendar, tap Settings, then Restore purchase in the Pro section. The paywall screen has the same button.

Pro is a non-consumable purchase, so restoring is free. If Family Sharing is on, family members can use it too. If Restore finds nothing, check that you are signed in with the right Apple Account and have an internet connection, then try again.

For refunds, Apple handles all payments: request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Questions

My night shift shows on the wrong day.

A shift belongs to the day it starts. Check the shift type's start and end times: an end time earlier than the start time means the shift ends the next morning.

How do I add the widget?

The widget is part of Pro. Touch and hold the Home Screen, tap Edit → Add Widget, search for Shift Calendar, and choose the small or medium size.

Is the pay estimate my real salary?

No. It is simple paid hours × your hourly rate, before tax and without allowances. Use it as a rough guide.

Reminders stopped after a few weeks.

iOS limits how many reminders an app can schedule, so Shift Calendar schedules about the next 30 days and refreshes them each time you open the app. Open it at least once a month.

Can I see more than one job?

Yes, with Pro: add more schedules and they are overlaid on one calendar.

Where is my data stored? Can I move it to a new iPhone?

Only on your iPhone. It moves to a new iPhone when you restore from an iPhone backup (iCloud Backup or a computer backup) or use Quick Start. There is no account or sync service.
