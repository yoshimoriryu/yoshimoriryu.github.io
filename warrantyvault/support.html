Warranty Vault: Receipts support

Receipts and warranty end dates, with on-device text reading.

What the app does

Warranty Vault keeps your receipts and tells you when each warranty ends. Scan a paper receipt or pick a photo; the app reads the text on your iPhone and suggests the store, purchase date and total for you to confirm. It lists items by the warranty that ends first and sends local reminders 30 and 7 days before the end date.

Free: Up to 5 items, with scanning, text reading and standard reminders.
Pro (one-time purchase, $2.99 in the US; local prices vary, no subscription): Unlimited items, custom reminder times and PDF export of an item with its receipt photos.

Works offline. No account, no ads, no tracking, no data collected. iPhone, iOS 17 or later.

Restore your Pro purchase

On a new iPhone, or after deleting and reinstalling the app:

  1. Sign in to the App Store with the same Apple Account you used to buy Pro.
  2. Open Warranty Vault, tap Settings, then Restore purchase in the Pro section. The paywall screen has the same button.

Pro is a non-consumable purchase, so restoring is free. If Family Sharing is on, family members can use it too. If Restore finds nothing, check that you are signed in with the right Apple Account and have an internet connection, then try again.

For refunds, Apple handles all payments: request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Questions

The scanned date or total is wrong. Why?

Text reading is a helper, not a guarantee. Faded, crumpled or unusual receipts can be misread. Always check the suggested values against the receipt and correct them before saving.

I don't get reminders.

Check that notifications are allowed: iPhone Settings → Notifications → Warranty Vault. The app's Settings screen also shows a notice when notifications are off. Reminders are only scheduled for warranties that have not ended yet.

Are my receipts backed up?

Receipts and photos are stored only on your iPhone and are included in your normal iPhone backup (iCloud Backup or a computer backup, if you use one). There is no separate Warranty Vault cloud. If you delete the app without a backup, the data is gone.

How do I send a receipt for a warranty claim?

Open the item and share its receipt photos. With Pro you can also export the item as one PDF with all details and photos.

Why can I only add 5 items?

The free version keeps up to 5 items. Pro, a one-time purchase, removes the limit.

Where is my data stored? Can I move it to a new iPhone?

Only on your iPhone. It moves to a new iPhone when you restore from an iPhone backup (iCloud Backup or a computer backup) or use Quick Start. There is no account or sync service.
