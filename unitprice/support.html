Unit Price: Compare & Save support

Price per kg, litre, oz or piece, including shelf deals.

What the app does

Unit Price compares products by their price per unit. Type the price and size of each item and it shows the price per 100 g, kg, litre, oz, fl oz or piece, and marks the cheapest. It starts with the shelf-tag unit used where you live and mixes units for you (grams against ounces, single items against multipacks).

Free: Compare up to 3 items.
Pro (one-time purchase, $1.99 in the US; local prices vary, no subscription): Unlimited items and deal math (buy 2 get 1, 2nd item half off, 3 for a fixed price, % off).

Works offline. No account, no ads, no tracking, no data collected. iPhone, iOS 17 or later.

Restore your Pro purchase

On a new iPhone, or after deleting and reinstalling the app:

  1. Sign in to the App Store with the same Apple Account you used to buy Pro.
  2. Open Unit Price, tap Settings, then Restore purchase in the Pro section. The paywall screen has the same button.

Pro is a non-consumable purchase, so restoring is free. If Family Sharing is on, family members can use it too. If Restore finds nothing, check that you are signed in with the right Apple Account and have an internet connection, then try again.

For refunds, Apple handles all payments: request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Questions

Which units does it support?

Weight (mg, g, ons, kg, oz, lb), volume (ml, L, fl oz, gallon), count (pieces, dozens) and length (cm, m, ft, yard). You can compare items in different units, and change the "per" unit (per 100 g, per kg, per oz and so on) from the main screen. The default units come from your region and can be changed in Settings.

How do multi-buy deals work?

With Pro, add a deal to an item: buy X get Y free, 2nd item at a percentage off, a number of items for a fixed price, or a flat percentage off. Unit Price then shows the price per unit you really pay when you buy the quantity the deal needs.

Why can I only add 3 items?

The free version compares up to 3 items. Pro, a one-time purchase, removes the limit.

Does it need internet?

No. Everything works offline. Only the purchase and Restore talk to Apple's App Store.

Where is my data stored? Can I move it to a new iPhone?

Only on your iPhone. It moves to a new iPhone when you restore from an iPhone backup (iCloud Backup or a computer backup) or use Quick Start. There is no account or sync service.
